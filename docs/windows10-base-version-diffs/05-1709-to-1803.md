# WinDiff analysis: Windows 10 core security binaries — 1709 → 1803 (amd64)

## Scope

- Old: Windows 10 1709 / BASE / `10.0.16299.15` · New: Windows 10 1803 / BASE / `10.0.17134.1` · Arch: `amd64`
- Binaries compared: `ntoskrnl.exe`, `ntdll.dll`, `win32k.sys`, `win32kbase.sys`, `win32kfull.sys`, `ci.dll`, `cng.sys`
- Focus: native and GUI syscall surface, speculative-execution hardening, VBS/DMA isolation, code integrity, telemetry, and cryptography

## Executive summary

- **[EDR] [anti-cheat] [vuln-research]** The dominant kernel change is the first large speculative-execution isolation wave in this series. The data directly adds KVA-shadow machinery, separate kernel/user page-table state, branch-prediction-barrier state, a process `SecurityDomain`, and a `RESTRICT_INDIRECT_BRANCH_PREDICTION` policy value. This is strongly consistent with the Meltdown/Spectre response, not an ordinary layout refactor.
- **[vuln-research]** `NtAllocateVirtualMemoryEx` and `NtMapViewOfSectionEx` appear in the kernel and as matching `Nt*`/`Zw*` exports and stubs in `ntdll`, creating two confirmed extended VM services. Their extra parameter/flag surface deserves validation-focused reversing.
- **[vuln-research]** The win32k shadow table expands by 54 services (four removals), concentrated in flip-object/composition transport, VAIL/Dxgk, input delegation, window groups, DPI conversion, and monitor control. The new handle- and message-heavy composition clusters are the most important new GUI attack surface.
- **[EDR] [anti-cheat]** `ci.dll` gains a coherent PPL/dynamic-code hardening cluster: process-create notification, debug-access policy, interpreter-based PPL-bypass mitigation, lockdown-mode instrumentation, and dynamic-code trust claims.
- **[EDR] [anti-cheat] [vuln-research]** New IOMMU/DMA-guard, VTL/VBS, enclave, and cache-isolation symbols indicate that device and process isolation were broadened alongside CPU-side speculative hardening.
- **[vuln-research]** `cng.sys` adds HKDF implementation and self-test symbols. This is cryptographic capability growth but not a new exported kernel API in this build.

## New syscalls

- **`NtAllocateVirtualMemoryEx`** [id 116] and **`NtMapViewOfSectionEx`** [id 269] — the kernel table adds both, while `ntdll` adds matching `Nt*` and `Zw*` exports/stubs. The `Ex` suffix indicates additional arguments or flags over the established VM allocation and section-mapping calls. Impact: confirmed user-reachable memory-manager surface and potentially new mapping/protection combinations. **[vuln-research]** Confirm by diffing the handlers against their non-`Ex` counterparts and fuzzing flag/extended-parameter validation.
- The kernel extractor also reports `PopPowerAggregatorValidateModernStandbyCallback` and `CmpCleanUpHigherLayerKcbCachesPreCallback` in the table. These are not `Nt*` services and have callback-shaped names, so they are treated as extraction/table-layout artifacts rather than user-callable syscalls.
- **win32k composition and flip transport (54 total new shadow services)** — the largest group includes `NtDCompositionCreateSynchronizationObject`, `NtDCompositionGetBatchId`, the `NtFlipObject*` producer/consumer family, token-manager flip-object handle creation, VAIL connect/disconnect/promotion, and present-to-hardware-queue submission. These services exchange handles, messages, surfaces, and synchronization objects across process/compositor boundaries, making lifetime, ownership, and size validation the priority. **[vuln-research]**
- **win32k input and window policy** — `NtMITDispatchCompletion`, `NtMITSetInputDelegationMode`, `NtMITSetLastInputRecipient`, window-group create/delete/set/policy calls, and bridge-window operations expand routing and cross-window policy state. These are interesting for sandbox/UI-boundary review because authorization depends on desktop, process, and window ownership. **[anti-cheat] [vuln-research]**
- **win32k display/DPI/device services** — new monitor brightness, process DPI, coordinate conversion, screen-capture cursor, device-removal support, and keyed-mutex/bundle-handle calls broaden lower-risk functional surface, with the handle-opening D3DKMT calls deserving the most scrutiny. **[vuln-research]**
- The hundreds of same-name syscall ID changes are table regeneration. They matter to direct-syscall tooling but are not new capabilities.

## New / changed security mitigations

- **`_PS_MITIGATION_OPTION::PS_MITIGATION_OPTION_RESTRICT_INDIRECT_BRANCH_PREDICTION`** (in `ntoskrnl.exe`, value `0x1c`) — direct evidence of a process policy controlling indirect-branch prediction. Together with `PspIsProcessSpecCtrlMitigated`, this is consistent with Spectre-v2 process isolation. **[EDR] [anti-cheat] [vuln-research]**
- **`_KPROCESS::CacheIsolationEnabled`** (bit 6 at offset `0x1b8`) and **`_EPROCESS::SecurityDomain`** (offset `0x838`) — direct process-state additions. `PspSetProcessCacheIsolationCallback`, `KiCheckAndApplyCacheIsolation`, and per-CPU `TrappedSecurityDomain` make a coherent security-domain/cache-isolation mechanism. The exact domain-transition policy remains an inference. **[anti-cheat] [vuln-research]**
- **`_KPROCESS::UserDirectoryTableBase`** (offset `0x278`) plus `_KPRCB::KernelDirectoryTableBase`, `RspBaseShadow`, `UserRspShadow`, and `ShadowFlags` — separate user/kernel translation and entry-state fields accompany `KiEnableKvaShadowing`, `MiInitializeShadowPageTable`, and shadow exception/syscall entry points. This is high-confidence KVA shadowing. **[all audiences]**
- **`_KPRCB::BpbState` bitfield** — records IBRS, STIBP, IBPB, SMEP, context-switch, idle, SMT, and user-to-user policy state. These are direct hardware/speculation-control indicators; the effective policy must be confirmed per CPU and registry/configuration path. **[vuln-research]**
- **`_MMSECURE_FLAGS::UserModeOnly` / `NoInherit`** — new secure-VM flags suggest finer control over protection scope and inheritance. Names and placement are factual; whether these are exposed through the new `Ex` VM calls requires handler analysis. **[vuln-research]**
- The resolved `_EPROCESS::MitigationFlagsValues` delta is only `Reserved : 1`; it is not counted as a named mitigation.

## New security-relevant features & components (beyond mitigations)

- **Code integrity and PPL self-protection** — `CiCreateProcessNotifyRoutine` pairs with an import of `PsSetCreateProcessNotifyRoutineEx`; `CiCheckProcessDebugAccessPolicy`, `CipMitigatePPLBypassThroughInterpreters`, `CiInstrumentPPLBypassBlocked`, and `CiPPLRevokedImageNotLoaded` show CI moving enforcement into process creation/debug/image decisions. `CiSetDynamicCodeTrustClaim` and `CipQueryDynamicCodeTrustClaim` add a trust-claim path, while lockdown-mode instrumentation provides policy telemetry. This is high-value for EDR PPL protection and bypass research. **[EDR] [anti-cheat] [vuln-research]**
- **Kernel coverage sampling and hardware trace support** — a large `EtwpCovSamp*` implementation cluster, `_ETW_IPT_SUPPORT`, `_ETW_HW_TRACE_EXT_INTERFACE`, and exported stack-walk APC controls add IP/stack sampling infrastructure. This is not the later `EtwTi` threat-intelligence channel, but it increases diagnostic/telemetry capability and privileged trace state worth hardening review. **[EDR] [vuln-research]**
- **DMA/VBS isolation** — `IoGetIommuInterface`, `_DMA_IOMMU_INTERFACE`, `_HAL_IOMMU_DISPATCH`, `HvlDmaGetDmaGuardEnabled`, and `VslGetSecurePciDeviceAlternateFunctionNumberForVtl0Dma` show new device-remapping and VTL-aware DMA control. `VslInitializeSecureKernelCfg` and IUM-key handling reinforce the VBS connection. **[EDR] [anti-cheat] [vuln-research]**
- **Enclave memory lifecycle** — `KeCreateEnclaveMetadataPage`, page-protection/change/block operations, and `MiHandleEnclaveFault` introduce a sizeable enclave-management cluster. This is both new protected-compute functionality and complex memory-manager attack surface. **[vuln-research]**
- **HKDF in `cng.sys`** — `SymCryptHkdf`, extract/expand/derive, and a self-test appear together. The absence of an export delta means this is likely consumed through an existing CNG dispatch path rather than a new direct driver export. **[vuln-research]**

## New attack surface (routines, callbacks, object types)

- **Extended VM operations** — the two confirmed native services add parsing paths for extended memory parameters and section mappings. **[vuln-research]**
- **Composition/flip/VAIL protocols** — the 1803 win32k expansion creates multiple stateful endpoint, token, shared-surface, message, and synchronization lifecycles. Review cross-process handle validation, cancellation/disconnect races, and producer/consumer ownership. **[vuln-research]**
- **IOMMU and enclave interfaces** — security boundaries are strengthened, but both clusters add privileged state machines. Drivers reaching the IOMMU interface and user processes reaching enclave APIs are useful fuzzing boundaries. **[vuln-research]**

## Notable structure changes

- `_EPROCESS` grows from `0x838` to `0x848` and adds `SecurityDomain` and `CoverageSamplerContext`, tying two major symbol clusters to persistent per-process state.
- `_KPROCESS` adds `UserDirectoryTableBase`, `AddressPolicy`, and `CacheIsolationEnabled`; `_KPRCB` grows sharply and acquires KVA-shadow and branch-prediction-barrier state.
- `_HEAP_LARGE_ALLOC_DATA` replaces spare bits with `GuardPageCount` and `GuardPageAlignment`, directly showing configurable guard pages for large heap allocations. **[vuln-research]**
- `_MMVAD` makes `VadFlags2` volatile and `_MMVAD_SHORT` gains a volatile long view. These are concurrency/layout changes, not standalone mitigations.

## Removed / deprecated

- win32k removes `NtDCompositionCurrentBatchId`, replacing it with `NtDCompositionGetBatchId`; this appears to be interface evolution rather than capability retirement.
- Device-lost support calls are replaced by process device-removal support calls, suggesting a semantic/generalization change in the graphics path.
- `NtSetCompositionSurfaceHDRMetaData` disappears from the extracted shadow table. Confirm whether HDR metadata moved behind another DComposition/Dxgk command before treating the feature as removed.

## Research leads

1. Reverse both `Ex` VM handlers and compare accepted extended-parameter types, privilege checks, and cleanup paths with the legacy services.
2. Trace `PspSetProcessCacheIsolationCallback`, `KiCheckAndApplyCacheIsolation`, and `KeQuerySpeculationControlInformation` to recover the exact per-process/domain transition policy.
3. Build state-machine fuzzers for the flip-object/VAIL endpoint and token-manager services, emphasizing disconnect/cancel and duplicated-handle races.
4. Follow CI's process notify callback into `CipMitigatePPLBypassThroughInterpreters` and dynamic-code trust-claim storage; confirm which interpreters, signers, and lockdown modes trigger denial versus instrumentation.
5. Reverse the `_DMA_IOMMU_INTERFACE` consumers and enclave page-state transitions to identify caller trust assumptions.
